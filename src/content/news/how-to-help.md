Campaigns at the justice-court level are won by neighbors talking to neighbors. If you can take a yard sign, walk a block, greet voters during early vote, or host a coffee in Bastrop, the campaign needs you.

Use the volunteer form on [Get Involved](/get-involved). Confirm you are in JP Precinct 1 with the [county precinct map](https://www.bastropcounty.gov/page/co.maps). And mark your own calendar: register by October 5, vote early October 19–30, or vote on Election Day, November 3.
