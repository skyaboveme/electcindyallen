Judge Cindy Allen is running to keep the Justice of the Peace, Precinct 1 bench in experienced hands. She has held this office since January 1, 2019, won re-election in 2022, and is the Republican nominee in the November 3, 2026 general election.

This website is a campaign communication. It is not the court’s filing portal, docket, or clerk line. If you have a case, a payment, or a question about a setting, use the [official Bastrop County JP Precinct 1 page](https://www.bastropcounty.gov/page/jp1).

If you live in Precinct 1 and want to help, start with [Get Involved](/get-involved). If you are ready to vote, the calendar is on the [Vote](/vote) page.
